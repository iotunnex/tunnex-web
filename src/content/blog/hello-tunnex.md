## Why self-host your VPN

Your network's control plane should answer to you, not to a vendor's uptime
page. Tunnex is built on a simple premise: **Connect everything. Trust
nothing.** Including us.

- One binary, any VPS with Docker and a public address
- WireGuard data plane, zero-trust policy on top
- The free Open tier keeps the VPN running forever

Curious what that costs? Short answer: [nothing, until you want
Enterprise](/pricing).

### A code block, styled like the docs

```bash
curl -fsSL https://get.tunnex.io | sh
tunnex up --domain vpn.example.com
```
